Concept

Safety & compliance

“Safe” means the system cannot take irreversible actions without policy and (usually) a human. “Compliant” means secrets, consent, platform rules, and audit trails are designed in — not bolted on.

Safety controls you will build

  • Policy gate — allow/deny by actor + action + resource
  • Operator auth — who may call the API / dashboard (see Policy vs operator auth)
  • HITL states — approve / changes_requested before publish
  • Budgets — max steps, tokens, wall time, revisions
  • Idempotent publish — retries do not double-post
  • Labeled fallbacks — never pretend canned text is a live model

Compliance checklist (practical)

AreaPractice
SecretsEncrypt at rest; never commit; redact from traces
OAuthReal consent; store least privilege; support disconnect
AuditLog denies, publishes, auth events (without raw tokens)
PlatformRespect Meta rate limits and content/app policies
DataKnow what PII you store; define retention if needed

Threat model (½ page you will write in Step 17)

  1. What assets matter? (tokens, brand voice, publish capability)
  2. Who might misuse them? (prompt injection, stolen env, buggy tool)
  3. What control stops each path? (policy, encryption, HITL, evals)
Remember

Prompt text that says “never publish without approval” is not a control. A policy function that returns deny — and a test that proves it — is a control.
